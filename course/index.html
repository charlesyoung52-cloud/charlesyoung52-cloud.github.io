Course Navigation & Study Guide

Official Orientation for Platforms Training Academy Online Unarmed Security (IDFPR 225 ILCS 447)

1

10 Core Curriculum Modules (20 Hours)

The course contains 10 in-depth modules covering Illinois criminal code, search & seizure, civil liability, use of force, restraints, PERC standards, fire safety, report writing, public relations, and emergency response. You must complete modules in sequential order (1 through 10).

2

The 3-Step Mastery Cycle in Every Module

Each module requires completing three distinct sections:
• Tab 1: Study Material & Field Narration: Read statutory case law and listen to Lead Instructor Charles Young's colorful real-world tactical case studies. Click "Mark Study Reviewed" when done.
• Tab 2: Tactical Scenario Simulator: Choose a response to realistic Chicago post dilemmas and review actionable legal/tactical feedback.
• Tab 3: Check on Learning: Answer all 3 questions correctly (100%). If you select an incorrect answer, Forced Remediation automatically jumps to the exact statutory section you need to review.

3

Progression Locks & Advance Rules

You cannot skip ahead to later lessons or take the final exam early. Module 2 unlocks only after Module 1 is 100% completed. The "Next Module" button remains locked with a lock badge until all three sections are satisfied.

4

Save Your Progress & Resume Anywhere

Your progress auto-saves continuously in your browser. You can click Save Progress in the top header at any time. In the Student Profile modal, you can export a progress backup file (.json) to resume your course on another computer, tablet, or phone without losing a single answer.

5

Official 50-Question State Qualification Exam

Once all 10 modules are mastered, the final exam unlocks. The exam consists of 50 multiple choice questions with a 75% minimum passing threshold (38/50 correct). You have unlimited retakes, an active stopwatch timer, category filter chips, and question flagging.
