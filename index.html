Illinois Concealed Carry Reciprocity

Illinois offers non-resident Concealed Carry Licenses (CCL) to qualified residents of states determined by the Illinois State Police to have substantially similar firearm training statutes:

Arkansas Idaho Mississippi Nevada Texas Virginia
