Illinois Concealed Carry Reciprocity

Illinois Concealed Carry License (CCL) holders enjoy recognition in reciprocating states nationwide. (Illinois State Police also issues non-resident Concealed Carry Licenses (CCL) to qualified applicants). Always verify current state-by-state reciprocity before traveling with a firearm:

Official State Portals

Illinois State Police (ISP)

www.ispfsb.com

FOID & Concealed Carry applications, applicant portal, FAQs, and authorized instructor verification.

Illinois General Assembly (ILGA)

www.ilga.gov

Full text of Illinois Compiled Statutes, the Firearm Owners Identification (FOID) Act, and the Concealed Carry Act.

Chicago Municipal Code

www.amlegal.com

Complete list of City of Chicago municipal ordinances and public transit safety regulations.

IDFPR • Division of Professional Regulation

idfpr.illinois.gov

Permanent Employee Registration Card (P.E.R.C.) verification, security guard roster checks, and statutory renewals.
